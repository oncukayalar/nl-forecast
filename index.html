"Days" is scored days / days with a forecast but missing actuals. Naive benchmarks use the same hour yesterday (latest fully known day at issue time) and the same hour last week. rMAE is the model MAE divided by the last-week MAE, the relative error used in the electricity price forecasting literature; below 1 is better than that naive forecast. It is given for the 7-day and 30-day windows. Day-ahead price MAE is the error of the day-ahead price of the same hour, taken as the forecast of the imbalance price; it uses only day-ahead prices that were published when the imbalance forecast was issued. Day-ahead price, scored days forecast by TimeH2O-v1 / by a fallback model: 1-day window 1 / 0, 7-day window 4 / 0, 30-day window 4 / 0. Method

Every day at 15:30 Europe/Amsterdam a job fetches data from the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform (day-ahead prices, load forecast, wind and solar forecasts, imbalance prices) and the morning's ECMWF weather forecast for ten points in the Netherlands and Germany (wind speed at 100 m, solar radiation, temperature), issues the forecasts above (the day-ahead forecast with TimeH2O-v1 (H2O.ai, checkpoint timeh2o-v1), a pretrained time-series model used without further training, with custom features built from the price history, the Dutch holiday calendar and the weather forecast for the target day; the imbalance forecasts with the mean of three models per target: LightGBM, a gradient-boosted tree model trained every day, and two pretrained time-series models used without further training, Chronos-2 (Amazon) and TimeH2O-v1 (H2O.ai, checkpoint timeh2o-v1)), fetches the actuals that have been published since, and rescores. All series are the hourly mean of the published values; since the day-ahead market moved to 15-minute products the day-ahead target is the hourly mean of the four 15-minute prices, stitched onto the hourly history before the switch. Imbalance targets are hourly means of TenneT's 15-minute settlement prices. The day-ahead forecast is issued two days ahead, before the auction clears; imbalance forecasts one day ahead. Forecasts are issued before market close and never edited: a missed run leaves a gap, nothing is backfilled.

On a day TimeH2O-v1 cannot make the day-ahead forecast, Chronos-2 or LightGBM makes it; the model label of the forecast shows which. The imbalance forecasts are this blend since 2026-11-18: the three models have equal weights, a model that cannot run on a day is left out, and the model label of each forecast lists the models that made it. TimeH2O-v1 is evaluated on this page live since 2026-11-14. This is an independent evaluation; the page is not affiliated with or endorsed by H2O.ai.
